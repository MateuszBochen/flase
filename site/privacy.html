Privacy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

This page explains what data the website flase.dev collects. It is about this website only - the Flase application you run yourself sends no data to us or to anybody else.

Analytics

With your consent we use Google Analytics 4 (Google Ireland Limited) to count visits and to see which parts of the page are useful. Google Analytics stores cookies (_ga, _ga_*) for up to 2 years and receives information about your visit: pages, time, approximate location, browser and device. IP addresses are not stored by Google Analytics 4. Data may be processed by Google outside of the EU under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Nothing is loaded or stored before you click Accept. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

Withdrawing consent

You can change your choice at any time with (also in the footer of the main page). After you reject, the analytics cookies of this website are removed.

Server logs

The web server keeps standard access logs (IP address, time, requested page, browser) for security and error diagnosis. They are not used for analytics and are deleted regularly.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict or object to processing and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in Poland: Prezes UODO). Questions: GitHub issues.
